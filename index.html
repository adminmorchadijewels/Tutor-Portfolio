About
"Most tutors teach your child what to think. I teach them how to think."

I'm Himanshu Gupta, an Engineering graduate with a B.Tech who works with students aged 13–18 across the UK and US. My approach is fundamentally different from conventional tutoring.

Rather than drilling answers, I focus on building independent thinkers — students who can tackle any subject, any exam, and any life challenge with clarity and confidence.

Engineering trained me to break complex problems into simple steps, find root causes, and think from first principles. I bring that same mindset to every session.
